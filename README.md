### About Me

PhD Candidate, M.Sc., and B.Sc. in Physics from the **Federal University of Rio Grande do Norte (UFRN, Brazil)**. My research sits at the intersection of **Computational Physics**, **Statistical Physics and Complex Systems**, **Theoretical Condensed Matter Physics**, **Generative Deep Learning** and **High-Performance Computing (HPC)**.

I focus on bridging first-principles mathematical modeling with scalable numerical solvers and parallel deep learning pipelines deployed on Linux/Slurm HPC clusters.

---

### Core Research Tracks

| Research Area | Methodology & Technical Scope |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Generative AI for Geophysics** | Domain translation between Distributed Acoustic Sensing (**DAS** across straight, helical, and hybrid fiber-optic cable geometries) and multicomponent Ocean Bottom Node (**OBN**) seismic data using generative models architectures in PyTorch, integrated with Optuna Bayesian optimization. |
| **Numerical Wave Propagation** | High-performance 2D elastic wave equation solvers in **Julia** using staggered-grid finite differences, fluid-solid interface coupling and synthetic seismic dataset generation. |
| **Theoretical Condensed Matter Physics** | Parallelized impurity concentration inversion and electronic transport simulations in large-scale hexagonal graphene flakes via the **Recursive Green's Function (RGF)** method and **Landauer-Büttiker** formalism. |
| **Seismic Signal Processing** | 2D seismic data interpolation and regularization via *Matching Pursuit Fourier Interpolation* (**MPFI**) and spectral analysis. |
